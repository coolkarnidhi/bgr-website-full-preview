Board of Directors

Arjun Govind Raghupathy
Chairman & Managing Director

Arjun Raghupathy is a graduate in Mechanical Engineering from Anna University and the son of the Founder, B. G. Raghupathy. He joined the Company in 2011, initially engaged in the business development of large-value EPC and BoP contracts, project monitoring and site construction.

He was subsequently appointed Managing Director of GEA-BGR Energy System India Limited, where he was responsible for business development, sales, contract delivery, client interaction, financial management, operations control and human resources.

He was inducted to the Board and designated Deputy Managing Director and Chief Operating Officer with effect from November 11, 2016, and was re-designated Managing Director on June 30, 2020. Pursuant to a special resolution passed by the members at the Annual General Meeting held on September 22, 2026, amending the Articles of Association, he was elevated to Chairman of the Board, and he currently serves as Chairman and Managing Director of the Company.

Ganesan Jeyakrishna
Executive Director & President, HR

Ganesan Jeyakrishna is a graduate in Business Administration from Madurai Kamaraj University (1985) and holds a Post Graduate Diploma in Personnel Management and Industrial Relations from Annamalai University (1987).

An HR professional with over three decades of experience, he spent 11 years with Manali Petrochemicals Ltd and SPIC Electric Power Corporation Ltd (SPIC group), where he held various positions. He has been associated with BGR for over 20 years and currently holds the position of President, HR.

Krishnamoorthi Meyyanathan
Independent Director

Krishnamoorthi Meyyanathan holds a B.G.L. from Madurai Kamarajar University and is a Fellow Member of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India, New Delhi. He has been a senior practising Company Secretary in Chennai for the past 14 years, following close to 30 years of experience with reputed Tamil Nadu State Government Public Sector Undertakings, including Tamil Nadu Cements Corporation Ltd, TAPCO and AAVIN, retiring as Senior Company Secretary in the DGM cadre in January 2010.

His experience spans secretarial practice, accounts, finance and banking, administration, budgeting and control, inventory control, and general management.

Surilisubbu Vasudevan
Independent Director

Surilisubbu Vasudevan is a Fellow Member of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India, a Post Graduate in Commerce and a Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA). He holds an LL.B. and an M.L. in International Law and Constitution from the University of Madras, and is pursuing a PhD in Corporate Restructuring (Mergers and Acquisitions) at Vels University, Chennai.

He brings over three decades of managerial experience spanning core accounting and MIS, including 25 years of post-qualification experience across the textile, garment, steel, power, service and IT infrastructure industries. His expertise covers finance, accounts, secretarial management, imports and exports, statutory compliance, debt restructuring, and resolution matters before the DRT, SARFAESI and NCLT forums.

Narmadha Dinakaran
Independent Director

Narmadha Dinakaran is a qualified Company Secretary with a Master’s degree in Corporate Secretaryship. She brings over 13 years of diverse experience in corporate affairs and board-level governance.

She served as an Independent Director at Mobis India Limited for more than seven years in the automobile components and ancillaries sector, and has held key roles at Exterran Energy Solutions, Sheenlac Paints and CMC Limited.

02

Management team

Sundar Srinivasan
Chief Financial Officer, Company Secretary & Compliance Officer

Sundar Srinivasan has been with BGR Energy Systems since June 2024 and currently holds the position of Company Secretary and Chief Financial Officer. He has over 33 years of experience in finance, accounting, company secretarial affairs and corporate compliance.

He is a qualified Company Secretary (ACS, 1993) and Cost Accountant (1987), with strong expertise in handling secretarial and compliance matters, together with experience across the finance and accounts functions.

Prior to joining BGR, he worked with S V Global Mill Limited, Turbo Energy Limited, LifeCell International Pvt. Ltd., Helios Estate Pvt. Ltd. and United India Insurance Company Limited.

Narendran Subramaniam
President – Strategy, Execution & Transformation

Narendran Subramaniam joined BGR Energy Systems Limited in March 2026 as President – Strategy, Execution & Transformation. He leads the Company’s financial and operational transformation and is responsible for corporate planning.

He brings over 30 years of experience across banking and consulting, with deep specialisation in operational excellence and recognised expertise in Lean and the Theory of Constraints. Prior to BGR, he has worked with Aurobindo Pharmaceuticals Limited, McKinsey & Company, Siemens, Tech Mahindra, Renoir Consulting and ICICI Bank.

He graduated in Economics from Loyola College, Chennai, where he was awarded the Gold Medal. He holds a Post Graduate Diploma in Business Management from the Indian Institute of Planning and Management, New Delhi, and has completed an executive programme in management at Columbia Business School, New York.

C. K. Ravi
Executive Director, E&C Business

C. K. Ravi has been with BGR Energy Systems Limited since December 2008 and currently holds the position of Executive Director, E&C Business.

He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering (1981) and has held ASNT Level II certification in the major non-destructive testing methods since 1986. He also holds a Post Graduate Diploma in Business Administration (2004) and is an assessor under the Business Excellence Program.

He brings considerable experience in the project management and operation of large thermal and hydro power plants, and also heads the Electrical Projects Division, which operates in the transmission and distribution of power.

He has 44 years of experience in the power sector, including 18 years with BGR Energy Systems Limited. Prior to joining BGR, he spent nearly 26 years with Tata Power.

Rangarajan Mukunthan
Chief Operating Officer, Product Business Division

Rangarajan Mukunthan joined BGR Energy Systems Limited in June 2026 as Chief Operating Officer of the Product Business Division. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering (2002), a Master of Science in Operations Management (2004) and an MBA in Finance and Marketing (2012).

He brings 35 years of experience to BGR, with extensive expertise across Operations, Manufacturing, Industrial Engineering and Business Management.

Prior to joining BGR, he held senior positions with Emerson India, Sanmar Engineering, Stanadyne Amalgamation Pvt. Ltd. and TVS Motor Company.

Ram Naresh Singh Tomar
President & Head, BTG Business

Ram Naresh Singh Tomar has been with BGR Energy Systems Limited since October 2012 and currently holds the position of President and Head, BTG Business, in addition to leading the joint venture companies, BGR Boilers Pvt. Ltd. and BGR Turbines Pvt. Ltd.

He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering (1980) and a Post Graduate Diploma in International Trade Law (2006).

He brings extensive knowledge and experience in managing and executing large-value contracts in the thermal power industry. Prior to joining BGR, he worked with major Indian companies, including NTPC Ltd. and Lanco Infratech Ltd. In a career spanning over 45 years, he brings a wealth of specialised knowledge across procurement and contracts, project management, claim management, dispute resolution, and the policies and systems governing multilaterally funded projects.

Sanjay Bhattacharya
President, Project Management

Sanjay Bhattacharya has been with BGR Energy Systems Limited since December 2016 and currently holds the position of President, Project Management.

He holds a Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering (1982) and brings vast experience in executing and managing large-value contracts in the thermal power industry.

He has over 44 years of experience in project execution and management, operations and maintenance, and engineering across the power industry. Prior to joining BGR, he worked with Tata Power for 30 years, with Power Mech Projects Ltd for two years, where he developed its operations and maintenance business, and with Jindal India Thermal Power Limited for three years as Chief Executive Officer (Technical) for its thermal power venture.

J. K. Naghusha
President, Commercial

J. K. Naghusha has been with BGR Energy Systems Limited since October 1988 and currently holds the position of President, Commercial.

A commerce graduate with more than 40 years of experience, he brings strong expertise across finance and accounts, procurement and commercial activities.
