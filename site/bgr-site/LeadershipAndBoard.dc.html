Board of Directors

Mr. Arjun Govind Raghupathy
Managing Director

A Mechanical Engineering graduate of Anna University, he joined BGR in 2011 and is responsible for business development, sales, contract delivery, client interaction, financial management, operations control and human resources. Appointed Managing Director in November 2016 and reappointed in 2021. Shareholders reappointed him for a further five-year term from 11 November 2026. Member of the Audit, Stakeholders Relationship and CSR Committees.

Mrs. Sasikala Raghupathy
Non-Executive Director & Chairperson

On the Board since 1985. Chairperson of the CSR Committee and a member of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.

Mr. Ganesan Jeyakrishna
Executive Director

Graduate in Business Administration (Madurai Kamaraj University, 1985) with a Post Graduate Diploma in Personnel Management and Industrial Relations (Annamalai University, 1987). Spent 11 years at Manali Petrochemicals and SPIC Electric Power Corporation. Member of the Stakeholders Relationship Committee.

Mr. Krishnamoorthi Meyyanathan
Independent Director

Practising Company Secretary. Appointed May 2024 and reappointed to May 2030. Chairman of the Audit, Nomination and Remuneration, and Stakeholders Relationship Committees.

Mr. Surilisubbu Vasudevan
Independent Director

Postgraduate in Commerce and a Chartered Financial Analyst. Appointed May 2024 and reappointed to May 2030. Member of the Audit, Nomination and Remuneration, and CSR Committees.

Ms. Narmadha Dinakaran
Independent Director

Master's degree in Corporate Secretaryship. Appointed March 2025 for a term to March 2030. Member of the Audit Committee.
